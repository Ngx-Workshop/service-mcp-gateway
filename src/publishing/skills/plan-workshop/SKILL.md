Define prerequisites and measurable objectives before authoring. In learningPlan map each objective to zero-based page, lab and assessment-question indexes. Every objective must be taught, practiced and assessed; indexes must reference real resources. Keep the difficulty and scope appropriate to the brief. Teach only what the supplied sources support; flag uncertainty through clear wording rather than inventing facts.
